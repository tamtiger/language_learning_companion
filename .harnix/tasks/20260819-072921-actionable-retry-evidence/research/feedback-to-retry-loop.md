# Research — Feedback-to-retry loop

Ngày 2026-08-19.

- CEFR Online interaction: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/online-interaction — action-oriented descriptors theo tình huống thật, hỗ trợ authentic transfer evidence.
- CEFR Companion Volume: https://book.coe.int/en/education-and-modern-languages/8152-common-european-framework-of-reference-for-languages-learning-teaching-assessment-companion-volume.html — production, interaction, mediation, self-assessment và qualitative descriptors.
- Lambert, Kormos & Minn, DOI 10.1017/S0272263116000085: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/abs/task-repetition-and-second-language-speech-processing/0EA95A4C7D9E90CD2AB30043F84A4635 — 32 learners, oral task repetition gắn với fluency gains; giới hạn: mẫu nhỏ, oral monologue, immediate gain.
- Xiao & Yang, DOI 10.1016/j.system.2019.01.004: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0346251X18301076 — qualitative study 2 teachers/16 EFL students: goal setting, self-evaluation, feedback, further action hỗ trợ self-regulation; giới hạn: teacher-guided classroom, không causal solo-app proof.
